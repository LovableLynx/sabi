# Sabi demo video: script for Loom (about 4 minutes)

The **SAY** lines are short on purpose. Say them slowly. Pause between lines. It is fine to stop and breathe. While you click, you don't need to talk.

---

## Before you press record

1. Open the app with the live AI in an incognito window:
   `https://sabi-app-wine.vercel.app/?api=<your Kaggle tunnel link>`
2. In a normal window, send one message in Ask Sabi so the AI is warmed up.
3. Have a second tab open on the results: `https://github.com/LovableLynx/sabi#evaluation`
4. Zoom the browser to 125%. Turn sound ON.
5. In Loom, choose Screen + Camera.

---

## 1. Hello (0:00 to 0:30)

**DO:** Start on the Sabi Arena welcome screen.

**SAY:**
"Hi, we're Team Sabi.

Many Nigerian students now study with AI chatbots.

But chatbots just give the answer.

Students copy it, and then fail in the exam hall.

So we built Sabi. A tutor that helps you think, not copy."

## 2. What Sabi is (0:30 to 0:50)

**SAY:**
"Sabi is built on N-ATLAS, Nigeria's own AI model.

We trained it to guide students with questions and Nigerian examples."

## 3. Signing up (0:50 to 1:20)

**DO:** Type Chidi and Okafor. Pick the parrot and tap it. Tap SS. Pick a state and a school. Tick the box. Press go.

**SAY** (while the form is on screen):
"Students pick their class: Primary, JSS or SS.

Other players only see 'Chidi O.'

The full name stays on the phone."

## 4. The route (1:20 to 1:40)

**DO:** Set the exam date and press Save. Point at "10 tasks".

**SAY:**
"Students set their exam date and count down the days.

Each bus stop has ten questions."

## 5. Asking Sabi (1:40 to 2:40)

**DO:** Open the first bus stop. Tap Ask Sabi. Type: *Abeg just tell me the answer*

**SAY:**
"Watch what happens when I beg for the answer."

**DO:** Wait for the reply. Tap Listen.

**SAY:**
"Sabi doesn't give it to me.

It uses a danfo example and asks me a question.

And it can read out loud."

**DO:** Close the chat. Pick the right answer. Lock it in.

**SAY:**
"Answers are always checked with an answer key, not the AI."

## 6. Exam mode (2:40 to 3:05)

**DO:** Close the lesson, open it again, and turn on Exam mode. Show the timer.

**SAY:**
"For exam practice, students turn on exam mode.

One timer. No hints.

At the end, they see what they got wrong, and why."

## 7. The results (3:05 to 3:40)

**DO:** Switch to the GitHub results tab.

**SAY:**
"We tested Sabi against the original N-ATLAS.

Normal N-ATLAS gave away the answer thirty-two percent of the time.

Sabi, only two percent.

When students were stuck, Sabi helped them correctly ninety-six percent of the time."

## 8. Close (3:40 to 4:00)

**DO:** Go back to the app.

**SAY:**
"Sabi works on a cheap phone, even offline.

Next, we want to test it in real classrooms.

Sabi doesn't give you the answer. It helps you sabi it.

Thank you."

**DO:** Stop the recording.

---

## If something goes wrong

- **"Waking Sabi up"** shows: just wait quietly. The first reply can take up to a minute. You can trim the wait in Loom later.
- **You stumble on a line:** pause, then say it again. Trim the mistake in Loom afterwards.
- **The tunnel dies:** restart the Kaggle cell and send Claude the new link.
